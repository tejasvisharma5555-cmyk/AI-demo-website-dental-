# Knowledge base — Luxe Hair Salon

> **DEMO DATA.** Luxe Hair Salon is a fictional business. Every price, stylist, hour, and policy
> below is fictional demo data supplied by the owner on 2026-11-06. There is no real address,
> phone number, or website.

**Q: What are your hours?**
A: Tuesday to Friday 9 AM to 7 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM, Sunday 10 AM to 4 PM. Closed Mondays.
All times are New York time.

**Q: Are you open on holidays?**
A: We're closed on major US holidays: Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day, Memorial Day,
Independence Day, and Labor Day.

**Q: Where are you located?**
A: We're in Huntington, New York. The team can confirm the exact address when you book.

**Q: Is there parking?**
A: The salon team can confirm parking details for you.

**Q: What services do you offer, and how much are they?**
A: Prices are starting prices; colour depends on hair length and thickness.

| Service | Price | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Women's haircut and styling | from $65 | about 60 minutes |
| Men's haircut | from $35 | about 30 minutes |
| Blowout | from $45 | about 45 minutes |
| Root touch-up | from $85 | about 90 minutes |
| All-over colour | from $120 | about 2 hours |
| Partial highlights | from $150 | about 2.5 hours |
| Full highlights | from $200 | about 3 hours |
| Balayage | from $225 | about 3 hours |
| Deep conditioning treatment | from $40 | about 30 minutes |
| Colour consultation | free | 15 minutes |

**Q: Who are your stylists?**
A: Emma does haircuts and styling (women's cuts, men's cuts, blowouts). Olivia does colour and
highlights (root touch-ups, all-over colour, partial and full highlights, colour
consultations). Sophia does balayage and treatments (balayage, deep conditioning).

**Q: Can I book with a specific stylist?**
A: Yes. Each service is done by its specialist stylist, so you can ask for them or just take the
next available appointment.

**Q: Do I need a consultation before colour?**
A: For new colour clients, we recommend a free 15-minute colour consultation before a major
colour change.

**Q: Do I need a patch test?**
A: The salon team will confirm whether a patch test is needed for your service.

**Q: What's your cancellation policy?**
A: We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. There's no cancellation fee.

**Q: Do I need to pay a deposit?**
A: No, deposits aren't required.

**Q: What if I'm running late?**
A: If you're more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment.

**Q: How do I reach a person?**
A: I can take a message and the salon team will follow up within one business day.
